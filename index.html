Questions

Where can I book?

Panglao (Bohol) is the first area with online bookings. Across the rest of the Philippines you can already browse dive sites and contact dive centres directly.

How do I pay?

You pay a deposit of at least 50% by card or GCash to confirm your spot. The rest is paid in the app or in cash at the centre.

What if the sea is too rough?

If the centre cancels the trip because of sea conditions, everything you paid is refunded.

Do I need an account?

You can browse trips, dive sites and centres without one. To book or keep a logbook, create a free account.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from Bookings in the app until the trip starts. Cancel 24 hours or more before and you get back everything you paid. With less than 24 hours, the centre keeps 50% of the trip price.

What level do I need?

Each trip shows its minimum level, such as Open Water or Advanced. Add your certification to your profile and bring your card: the centre checks it on the day. Not certified yet? Contact the centre about try dives and courses.

Is there a medical form?

Yes. Before your first booking you fill in the standard diver medical questionnaire used by PADI and SSI. It is valid for 12 months. If you answer yes to any question, you need a doctor's signed clearance.
